| Servings   | 4-6       |
| Prep       | 5 min     |
| Cook       | 25-40 min |
| Total Time | 30-60 min |

## Ingredients


### Main

|       |             |                                   |
|:----- |:----------- |:--------------------------------- |
| 1     | pound       | ground beef                       |
| 1-2   | cups        | frozen corn (or 1 can)            |
| 1     | 15 0z can   | tomato sauce                      |
| 2-3   | 15 oz can   | kidney beans (drained)            |

### Spices
|       |             |                                   |
|:----- |:----------- |:--------------------------------- |
| 1 1/2 | tablespoons | chili powder                      |
| 1/2   | tablespoons | ground cumin                      |
| 1     | tablespoon  | minced dried onion                |
| 1/2   | tablespoon  | dried oregano                     |
| 1/2   | tablespoon  | dried basil                       |
| 1     | teaspoon    | granulated dried garlic           |
| 1     | teaspoon    | cocoa powder (optional)           |
| 1/2   | teaspoon    | crushed red pepper                |
| 1/8   | teaspoon    | cayenne pepper (or more to taste) |

## Directions

1. Everything cooks in a large skillet.
2. On medium-high to high stove, brown ground beef. Drain if using high fat ground beef.
3. Reduce heat to medium. Combine all spices with meat and stir/mix to distribute spices evenly. (You can reserve the oregano, basil, and crushed red pepper to add with the tomato sauce later.)
4. Add the frozen corn. Cook for a few minutes, stirring until the corn is fully thawed.
5. Add the tomato sauce and heat until simmering, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in the kidney beans and heat until simmering resumes. Reduce heat to low and simmer/heat through for 20–40 minutes.

## Notes

-   **For soupier chili**, add a second can of tomato sauce or don't drain the kidney beans.
-   **Salt**, I don't use salt in the recipe, but you could.
